Privacy Policy

StoreHouse · Last updated: 4 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StoreHouse (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information in the StoreHouse app for iPhone, iPad and Mac (the “App”). In short: your reading stays with you. We don’t run servers, there’s no account, and we can’t see anything you do in the App.

1. Summary

  • We collect nothing. No data from the App is sent to us.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. The App has no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • Your data stays yours. Your reading history and plans are kept on your device and in your own iCloud account. Only you can get to them.

2. What the App keeps

  • Reading history: the chapters, or parts of chapters, you mark as read, when you read them, and how they were marked (opened from the App, ticked off by hand, or marked as read earlier).
  • Reading plans: the plans you start, when you started them and your pace. For a plan you make yourself, also the books it covers and its finish date.
  • Settings: your reading mode, whether the Live Activity is shown, whether the Mac menu bar item and Dock count are shown, and whether, when and how often the daily reminder comes. Settings stay on the device you set them on.

The App never asks for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or anything else about you.

3. iCloud sync

If you’re signed in to iCloud, your reading history and plans sync between your devices through Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit). They’re kept in the private database of your own iCloud account. We can’t see that data. Apple’s handling of it is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for StoreHouse in the iCloud section of the Settings app, or of System Settings on a Mac. To keep your devices up to date, the App may receive silent notifications from iCloud. These never contain messages from us.

4. Widgets, Live Activities, the menu bar and reminders

  • The widget and the Live Activity show today’s reading. The App passes it to them on your device. Anyone who can see your Home Screen, Lock Screen or Mac desktop can see them.
  • On a Mac, the menu bar item shows today’s reading and how far along it is, and the Dock icon can count what’s left to read. Anyone who can see your screen can see them.
  • Daily reminders are scheduled on your device. They name the day’s reading and can show on your Lock Screen. You can choose how they appear in Settings › Notifications › StoreHouse, or System Settings › Notifications › StoreHouse on a Mac.
  • We don’t send push notifications.

5. Opening a passage

The App doesn’t include any Bible text. Opening a passage opens it in the JW Library app or, if that isn’t installed, on jw.org in your browser. The link says which passage to open and nothing else. That app and website are run by others under their own privacy policies. We don’t control what they collect. On a Mac, you can also share a passage, which passes its jw.org link to the app you share it with.

6. Information from Apple

If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, or System Settings on a Mac), Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics for the App that don’t identify you. Apple also tells us how many times the App has been downloaded. We use this only to fix and improve the App.

7. Deleting your data

  • Erase All Data, in the App’s Settings, deletes your reading history and plans from that device, from iCloud and from your other devices, and resets your settings. This can’t be undone.
  • Deleting the App removes its data from that device. The copy in iCloud stays until you erase it in the App, or delete it in the iCloud storage section of the Settings app, or of System Settings on a Mac.
  • Exported files: on a Mac, Export Reading History saves your reading history as a file where you choose. It isn’t sent to us. Erase All Data doesn’t remove it, so delete it yourself if you no longer want it.
  • Device backups (iCloud Backup, Time Machine, or a backup to your computer) may include the App’s data, depending on your backup settings.

8. Children

The App doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, children included.

9. Your rights (EU/EEA including Ireland, and UK)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data. We don’t hold any. You can see, change and delete everything the App keeps from within the App. Data in iCloud is held by Apple; the Apple Privacy Policy explains how to exercise your rights with Apple. For anything else, contact daragh.dev@icloud.com.

10. Security

Data on your device is protected by your device’s passcode or login password and encryption, and data in iCloud by Apple’s iCloud security. Use a passcode or password on your device and keep its software up to date.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes or the law requires it. Changes will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date.
