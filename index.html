Getting started

How do I choose a reading plan?

Tap Choose a Plan, then pick one:

  • Yearly: the whole Bible in a year, book by book or in the order events happened.
  • Memorial: the events before and after the Memorial, each day’s reading on the date it happened.
  • By Topic: one part of the Bible, or one account followed through it, such as Israel’s history or Jesus’ life.
  • Choose the Books: make your own plan from any books you like.

Then choose how fast: a day at a time, a number of chapters a day, by a finish date, or at your own pace.

Can I follow more than one plan?

Yes. Tap Add a Plan to read another beside the first. Each keeps its own pace.

Where’s the Bible text?

StoreHouse doesn’t include any Bible text. Tapping a passage opens it in the JW Library app or, if that isn’t installed, on jw.org, and marks it as read.

Reading and streaks

How do I mark something as read without opening it?

Swipe left on a passage and tap Read, or touch and hold it for more options. To undo, swipe again and tap Unread.

Read some of the Bible before you started using StoreHouse? Mark those chapters or whole books as read and they’ll count toward your plans and stats.

I missed a day. Is my streak gone?

Not yet. Read two days’ worth the next day and the missed day counts, so your streak carries on. Two days missed in a row can’t be made up.

What’s the difference between starting over and stopping a plan?

Start Over clears that plan’s checkmarks so you can read it again. Stop Following This Plan keeps your place for later. Either way, your reading history and streaks stay.

iCloud sync

How does sync work?

Your reading history and plans sync through your own iCloud account to every iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. There’s nothing to set up and no StoreHouse account.

Settings such as the daily reminder and the Live Activity are set on each device separately.

My reading isn’t showing up on my other device.
  • Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • Check that iCloud is on for StoreHouse, in the iCloud section of the Settings app, or of System Settings on a Mac.
  • Open StoreHouse on both devices while they’re online, and give it a minute to catch up.

Reminders, Lock Screen and widgets

How do I get a daily reminder?

In StoreHouse’s Settings, turn on Daily Reminder and pick a time. It names today’s reading, and doesn’t come if you’ve already read. Choose Remind Again to have it come back every few minutes until you have.

My reminder isn’t arriving.
  • Make sure notifications are allowed in Settings › Notifications › StoreHouse, or System Settings › Notifications › StoreHouse on a Mac.
  • Check that a Focus isn’t silencing them.
  • The reminder doesn’t come on days you’ve already read.
How do I show today’s reading on the Lock Screen?

Turn on Live Activity in StoreHouse’s Settings. It’s off until you turn it on. Today’s reading then shows on the Lock Screen and, on iPhones that have one, in the Dynamic Island. It’s shown for plans read a day at a time.

If StoreHouse says Live Activities are turned off, allow them in Settings › StoreHouse.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, and search for StoreHouse.

  • Today’s Reading shows what’s left to read today, or what’s next in your plan.
  • Streak shows how many days in a row you’ve read, and this week’s reading.
  • Read Next is a control for Control Centre or the Lock Screen that opens what’s next in your Bible app.

On a Mac

Can I see today’s reading without opening the app?

Yes. StoreHouse can show today’s reading in the menu bar and count what’s left on its Dock icon. Turn either on or off in StoreHouse’s Settings.

Can I export my reading history?

Yes. Choose File › Export Reading History… (Shift-Command-E) and pick where to save it.

Your data

Who can see my reading?

Only you. There’s no account, no ads and no tracking, and nothing is sent to us. Your reading is kept on your devices and in your own iCloud account. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete everything?

Erase All Data, in StoreHouse’s Settings, deletes your reading history and plans on all your devices and in iCloud, and resets your settings. This can’t be undone.
